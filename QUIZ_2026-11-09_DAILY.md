### Q2 - conduction versus conductor

Original: `Heat moved from the hot end of the metal spoon to its handle by ______.`

Learner: B. `conductor`. Correct: A. `conduction`.

The sentence asks how heat moved: the heat-transfer process is conduction.
A conductor is the material or object that carries heat/electricity, such as
the metal spoon. Both choices are nouns, so context and role decide.
Q1 correctly identified the wire as a conductor; Q2 reverses those roles again.
Memory: `The spoon is a conductor; heat travels through it by conduction.`
