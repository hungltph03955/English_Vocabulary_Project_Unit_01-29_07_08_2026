### Q8 - illogical versus logically

Original: `The rule is ______: it requires everyone to attend the meeting but forbids everyone from entering the room.`

Learner: C. `logically`. Correct: B. `illogical`.

The blank describes the rule after linking verb is, so it needs an adjective.
The requirement to attend and ban on entering contradict each other: illogical
means unreasonable or inconsistent. Logically is an adverb meaning in a
reasoned way; it cannot describe the rule in this sentence.
Memory: `The rule is illogical; she explained the problem logically.`

### Q11 - graphic versus bibliography

Original: `The report includes a ______ showing the stages of the process with arrows and icons instead of a block of text.`

Learner: D. `bibliography`. Correct: A. `graphic`.

Both options are nouns, so meaning decides. Arrows and icons make a visual
illustration: a graphic. A bibliography lists books or other sources; it does
not illustrate process stages. Graphic has a noun sense as well as adjective
senses in the bank.
Memory: `The graphic shows the process; the bibliography lists the sources.`
