### Q1 - tolerating vs compounding

Original sentence:
- `______ chat interruptions all afternoon made deep work almost impossible.`

Learner answer:
- A. `Compounding`

Correct answer:
- C. `Tolerating`

Why:
- `tolerating` means enduring or putting up with something unpleasant.
- `compounding` means building up cumulatively, often making a problem worse over time.
- In this sentence, the subject is the action of putting up with interruptions all afternoon, so `tolerating` fits.

- `Tolerating constant interruptions drains focus.`
- `A compounding problem gets worse as each issue adds to the next.`
