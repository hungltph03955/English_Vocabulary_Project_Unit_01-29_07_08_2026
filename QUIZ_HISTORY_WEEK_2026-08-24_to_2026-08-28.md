### Q1 - `semicircle` vs `semicircular`

Original sentence:
- `The designer used a ______ seating layout so everyone could face the presenter.`

Learner answer:
- A. `semicircle`

Correct answer:
- B. `semicircular`

Why:
- `semicircle` is a noun: the half-circle shape.
- `semicircular` is an adjective: shaped like a semicircle.
- Because the blank describes `seating layout`, the sentence needs the adjective.

### Q10 - `provision` vs `protective`

Original sentence:
- `The contract includes a ______ for emergency support during the migration.`

Learner answer:
- C. `protective?`

Correct answer:
- B. `provision`

Why:
- `provision` is a noun meaning a supply, arrangement, or clause prepared for a future need.
- `protective` is an adjective meaning giving protection. It cannot fit naturally after `includes a`.
- A contract includes a `provision` for support.
