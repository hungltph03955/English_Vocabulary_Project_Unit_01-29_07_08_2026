### Q6 - conductive versus conduction

Original: `The engineer chose rubber for the handle because it is less electrically ______ than metal.`

Learner: A. `conduction`. Correct: B. `conductive`.

Why: `Is less electrically ...` requires adjective `conductive`.

### Q9 - flex versus flexor

Original: `The trainer asked her to ______ her ankle slowly to test its range of motion.`

Learner: B. `flexor`. Correct: C. `flex`.

Why: `Asked her to ...` requires a base-form verb. `Flex` means bend;
`flexor` is the muscle that bends a joint.

Memory: `Flex your ankle; the flexor helps bend the joint.`

### Q8 - transformation versus platform

Original: `The office's ______ from a paper-based workplace into a fully digital one took six months.`

Learner: D. `platform`. Correct: B. `transformation`.

Why: `From a paper-based workplace into a fully digital one` describes a major
change, and `took six months` measures its duration. `Transformation` (noun)
means the process or result of changing. `Platform` (noun) means a raised
surface or a system/foundation supporting activity, not the change process.
Both options are nouns; this is a meaning/context contrast rather than POS.

Memory: `The company's digital transformation introduced a new online platform.`
