## Focused Review

### Q4 - unfairly

Original sentence: `The landlord ______ kept part of the deposit even though the apartment was undamaged.`

Learner: B. `unfairness`.
Correct: C. `unfairly`.

Why: The blank modifies the verb kept, so it needs the adverb unfairly. Unfairness is a noun and cannot describe how the landlord kept the deposit.

Memory: `The landlord unfairly kept part of the deposit.`

### Q7 - overpay

Original sentence: `Before accepting the first quote, compare several contractors so you do not ______.`

Learner: D. `overlook`.
Correct: A. `overpay`.

Why: Comparing quotes helps you avoid paying more than necessary: overpay. Overlook means fail to notice or ignore something and normally needs an object.

Memory: `Compare several quotes so you do not overpay.`

### Q8 - observable

Original sentence: `The update produced an ______ reduction in loading time, measured consistently across five tests.`

Learner: A. `perceptive?`.
Correct: C. `observable`.

Why: The learner's meaning for perceptive (tinh y) is correct, but perceptive normally describes a person who notices details. A reduction measured across tests is observable.

Memory: `A perceptive analyst noticed an observable reduction.`

### Q9 - universe

Original sentence: `Through a telescope, astronomers study distant galaxies across the ______.`

Learner: A. `universal`.
Correct: B. `universe`.

Why: After the, the sentence needs the noun universe, meaning all space and its galaxies. Universal is an adjective meaning applying everywhere or to everyone.

Memory: `Astronomers study distant galaxies across the universe.`
