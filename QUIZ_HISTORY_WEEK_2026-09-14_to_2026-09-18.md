## Focused Review

### Q6 - transparently

Original sentence: `The manager explained the refund policy ______, showing customers every condition.`

Learner: B. `transparency`.
Correct: A. `transparently`.

Why: The blank describes how the manager explained the policy. Use adverb transparently; transparency names the quality and is a noun.

Memory: `The manager explained the policy transparently.`

### Q12 - form

Original sentence: `The designer changed the symbol's ______ from a square to a circle.`

Learner: B. `uniform`.
Correct: A. `form`.

Why: Changing a square to a circle changes its form (shape). Uniform means standard clothing or consistent; it does not name the symbol's shape here.

Memory: `The designer changed the symbol's form.`

### Q15 - unfairly

Original sentence: `The employee was treated ______: others doing the same work received twice her pay.`

Learner: A. `unfairness`.
Correct: D. `unfairly`.

Why: The blank describes how the employee was treated. Use adverb unfairly; unfairness is the noun meaning the quality of being unfair.

Memory: `The employee was treated unfairly.`

### Q22 - outweigh

Original sentence: `The long-term savings should ______ the initial cost of replacing the equipment.`

Learner: B. `overcharge?`.
Correct: C. `outweigh`.

Why: The savings should be greater or more important than the cost: outweigh. Overcharge means charge a customer too much. The learner identified the savings/cost comparison but chose a pricing verb.

Memory: `The savings outweigh the initial cost.`

### Q26 - obligatory

Original sentence: `Attendance is ______ for all new employees; anyone absent must complete the training later.`

Learner: A. `obvious`.
Correct: C. `obligatory`.

Why: Absent employees must still complete training, so attendance is required: obligatory. Obvious means clear or easy to see, not mandatory.

Memory: `Attendance at training is obligatory.`

### Q28 - underpass

Original sentence: `The pedestrian ______ takes people safely beneath the busy road.`

Learner: B. `underground`.
Correct: D. `underpass`.

Why: A passage beneath a road is an underpass. Underground describes below-ground location (and can name an underground railway); it is not the passage noun needed here.

Memory: `We crossed the road through the underpass.`
