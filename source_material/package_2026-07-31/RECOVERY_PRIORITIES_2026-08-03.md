# Recovery Priorities - Monday 03/08/2026

## P1 - Must appear early

### `illegible` vs `illiterate`

- `illegible`: handwriting/text cannot be read.
- `illiterate`: a person cannot read or write.

Prompt pattern:

- The handwriting was almost impossible to read, so it was ______.
- The adult education class helped ______ adults learn to read.

### `misuse` vs `mislead` vs `mismatch`

- `misuse`: use something in the wrong way or for the wrong purpose.
- `mislead`: make someone believe something false.
- `mismatch`: two things do not match.

Prompt pattern:

- The company was criticized for ______ customer data.
- The advertisement ______ customers.
- The two versions created a configuration ______.

### `objective` vs `observant` vs `observable`

- `objective`: fair, factual, not biased.
- `observant`: good at noticing details.
- `observable`: able to be seen or measured.

Prompt pattern:

- A researcher should remain ______.
- The guard was ______ and noticed the unattended bag.
- The effect was not directly ______.

### `mishandle` vs `misbehave`

- `mishandle`: handle or manage something badly.
- `misbehave`: behave badly.

Prompt pattern:

- The team ______ the incident by deleting useful logs.
- The student was asked not to ______ during the ceremony.

### `illogical` vs `irrelevant` vs `irrational`

- `illogical`: not following logic.
- `irrelevant`: not related.
- `irrational`: not based on reason; often emotional or unreasonable.

Prompt pattern:

- The conclusion was ______ because it did not follow from the evidence.
- The comment was ______ to the question.
- He made an ______ decision while angry.

## P2 - Include after P1

### `intersect`

Meaning: cross or meet at a point.

Prompt pattern:

### `microaggression`

Meaning: a subtle or indirect insulting remark/action, often about identity, background, race, gender, or ethnicity.

Prompt pattern:
