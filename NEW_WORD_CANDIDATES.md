# New Word Candidates

Date created: 2026-08-10

This file tracks incidental words that appeared in Quick questions, prompts, or
distractors and that the learner explicitly said they did not know.

These words are candidates only. They are not active vocabulary until the user
asks to promote them into `VOCABULARY_STATE.csv` and the relevant addendum.

## How to Use

When grading a quiz:

- If the learner says they do not know a word and that word is already in
  `VOCABULARY_STATE.csv`, treat it as Recovery or light watch.
- If the learner says they do not know a word and that word is not in
  `VOCABULARY_STATE.csv`, add it here as a candidate.
- Record the original quiz sentence or option where the word appeared.
- Add a short meaning if it is clear from context or from project material.
- Do not promote candidates into the active vocabulary bank without user
  confirmation.
