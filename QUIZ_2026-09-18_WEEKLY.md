   A. subtitle | B. substitution | C. substitute | D. subheading

18. Automatic data ______ keeps the phone and laptop copies of the calendar identical.
   A. synchronization | B. synchronize | C. synthesis | D. symphony

19. The orchestra performed a four-movement ______ at the evening concert.
   A. symphonic | B. sympathetic | C. symphony | D. sympathy

   A. translate | B. translation | C. translator | D. transparency

   A. overpay | B. overcharge | C. outweigh | D. overlook
