### Q3 - produce versus product

Original: `The new solar panels can ______ enough electricity to power the small office during the day.`

Learner: B. `product`. Correct: A. `produce`.

The modal `can` requires a base-form verb. `Produce` is the verb; `product` is
the noun for a thing made. Memory: `Solar panels produce electricity;
electricity is their product.`

### Q9 - chess versus bike

Original: `In ______, each player moves pieces on a checkered board and tries to trap the other player's king.`

Learner: D. `bike`. Correct: B. `chess`.

Pieces, a checkered board, and a king identify `chess`. The active Tactics
entry `bike` is a verb meaning ride a bicycle. Memory: `Play chess on a board;
bike along a road.`

### Q11 - logical versus illogical

Original: `Her troubleshooting plan was ______: test the simplest possible cause before replacing expensive parts.`

Learner: D. `illogical`. Correct: A. `logical`.

Testing the simplest cause first is sensible and ordered, so it is `logical`.
`Illogical` describes reasoning that does not make sense. Memory: `Testing
simple causes first is a logical troubleshooting plan.`
